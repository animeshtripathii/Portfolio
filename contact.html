Common questions about working with me and my services.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A basic website takes 2-3 weeks, while full-stack applications may take 4-8 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline after our initial discussion.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! I've worked with clients across the globe and am comfortable with different time zones. I use tools like Slack, Zoom, and Discord for seamless communication.
