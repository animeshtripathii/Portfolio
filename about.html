My Story

Hello! I'm Animesh Tripathi, a Full Stack Developer based in New Delhi, India. My journey into programming started when I was in high school, fascinated by how websites and applications work. That curiosity turned into a passion that has driven my career ever since.

I specialize in building scalable web applications and have experience working with startups and established companies alike. I believe in writing clean, maintainable code and creating user experiences that are both functional and delightful.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open-source projects, or sharing my knowledge through technical blog posts and mentoring.
